# Hospitality AI Briefing — 2026-11-23

## Operational AI

Across the restaurant sector, AI is cementing operational maturity across drive-through lanes, automated production lines, and scheduling. 
On Thursday, Anthropic debuted on public markets with a valuation well north of $1 trillion, heralding the maturity of the artificial intelligence sector — and in the last three months, no fewer than seven major restaurant companies have announced significant expansions of their AI toolsets.


**Checkers & Rally's** (20 Nov) — 
Hi Auto's voice ordering technology is now deployed at 267 Checkers & Rally's restaurants and is on track to reach half of the system by early next year.
 This expansion deepens an already substantial drive-thru voice AI rollout: 
Hi Auto's voice ordering tech processes 1 million orders a week across 500 Bojangles restaurants with 98% accuracy, the chain said.


**McDonald's** (20 Nov) — 
McDonald's aims to deploy bilingual drive-thru voice AI by the end of next year. The fast food giant says the automated ordering system could save 50 weekly labor hours, but some franchisees are unsure if the technology is something customers want or if it can improve hospitality.


**Taco Bell** (20 Nov) — 
Taco Bell plans to have similar ordering tech live in 1,500 stores by the end of 2026.
 Parent Yum Brands characterised the rollout as part of a broader tech-led recovery at KFC alongside continued Taco Bell marketing investment.

**Wonder** (20 Nov) — 
Wonder deployed its first instance of an automated makeline, based on tech it acquired from Sweetgreen.
 
The company will also begin delivering Create creations via drone within the next couple of months, and by end of 2027, Wonder Create will span 30 locations serving 30 million people.


Sources: [Restaurant Dive — "How 7 restaurant chains are using AI," Nov 20 2026](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/7-restaurant-chains-deploy-artificial-intelligence/875198/); [Nation's Restaurant News](https://www.nrn.com/restaurant-technology); [Restaurant Dive — Hi Auto / Checkers & Rally's expansion](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/checkers-rallys-expand-hi-auto-partnership/875082/)

So what: Drive-thru voice AI is exiting the pilot phase and entering system-wide rollout at multi-thousand-unit brands. Mid-market operators should treat this as a capability threshold: Bojangles' 98% accuracy at 1 million weekly orders indicates the technology is reliable enough for franchise accountability, and the McDonald's 50-hour-per-week labor-hour claim will accelerate franchisee pressure to deploy regardless of corporate timelines.
