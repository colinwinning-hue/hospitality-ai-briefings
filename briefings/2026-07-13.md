# Hospitality AI Briefing — 2026-07-13

# HOSPITALITY AI BRIEFING | July 13, 2026

The dominant story this cycle is the consolidation of the restaurant reservation market, with three ecosystem-scale acquisitions reshaping who controls the guest relationship at the moment of booking. Alongside that, Google's confirmed extension of agentic commerce to hotel booking and Booking Holdings' Q1 earnings confirmation add urgency to the hotel distribution debate. Toast shipped its most substantive product release since its IPO. And the labour crisis is spawning a dedicated AI software category.

---

## Funding & M&A

The restaurant reservation market has undergone its most consequential ownership restructuring in a decade across three simultaneous deals.


**American Express** is proposing to acquire **TheFork** from Tripadvisor for **$700 million**, which would add TheFork's network of more than 50,000 restaurants across 11 European countries to American Express' growing dining ecosystem. Combined with existing platforms Resy and Tock, the acquisition would expand American Express' network to approximately 75,000 bookable venues worldwide, creating one of the largest restaurant reservation and dining engagement platforms in the industry.
 
Separately, Resy is integrating 5,000 eateries, bars, and wineries from Tock onto its platform this summer — a move announced under parent company American Express' ownership of Tock. Meanwhile, DoorDash announced its $1.2 billion acquisition of SevenRooms, a reservation platform focused on direct bookings.



**DoorDash** and **SevenRooms** are framing the acquisition around cross-channel data continuity. SevenRooms co-founder Joel Montaniel put it directly: "Delivery and dine-in have typically been siloed data sets. If a customer has ordered six times and they're coming into the restaurant for the first time, are they a first-time customer or a seventh-time customer?"



**Hospitality tech funding** crossed **$1 billion** across 40 companies in the 12 months to March 2026, with the biggest 90-day burst between December 2025 and February 2026, when Mews ($300 million), Kindred ($125 million), and Limehome (€75 million) raised almost back-to-back. PMS companies took the largest overall share at **$408.1 million**. AI-led guest experience platforms — Duve, Chatlyn, Conduit, and Canary Technologies — raised a combined **$152.6 million**, targeting the need to deliver fast, personalised service with leaner teams.
 
19 of the 40 funded rounds were raised at pre-seed, seed, or Series A, compared with just four at Series C or D, indicating a market that is still being built.


Counterbalancing the Mews fundraise: **Mews cut 15% of its workforce** this cycle, citing AI changing the "economics of hospitality" — a signal that the PMS scale race is entering a cost-efficiency phase and headcount built during growth is now being exchanged for automation.

Also noted from the RTN news ticker this week: **Liberty Mutual backed inKind with $320 million** in restaurant financing; **GoTab acquired Fishbowl** to deepen restaurant guest marketing and loyalty; **Blink raised $17 million** and partnered with Shake Shack on frontline workforce tech.

Sources: [Restaurant Technology News](https://restauranttechnologynews.com/2026/06/american-express-expands-restaurant-technology-ambitions-with-700-million-thefork-acquisition/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/25/doordash-resy-opentable-restaurant-reservation-wars.html), [Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/report/4131877/hospitality-tech-drew-over-1-billion-in-the-last-year-with-pms-and-ai-led-platforms-capturing-biggest-share), [PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/news/technology/mews-job-cuts-15-per-cent)

**So what:** The reservation market has bifurcated into two competing ecosystems — DoorDash/SevenRooms for operators who want delivery-to-dine-in data continuity, and AmEx/Resy/Tock/TheFork for operators who want premium cardholder spend and European reach. For PE-backed restaurant groups, the ownership of your reservation system now determines which data flywheel you feed. This is a strategic alliance decision, not a software procurement one.

## AI Visibility & Discovery

The distribution funnel for both restaurants and hotels is developing a structural two-layer architecture, with AI handling discovery and established platforms capturing conversion.


**DoorDash** launched **Ask DoorDash** on June 11, an in-app AI chatbot that lets users order food and groceries with text prompts and photos, and book restaurant reservations using natural language. "Traditional search works best when you know the exact restaurant or table you're looking for," DoorDash wrote. "Ask DoorDash is designed for the moments when you don't." DoorDash's app can build a grocery cart based on a photo from a cookbook, a picture of a grocery list, or a recipe — and for reservations, users can ask for "a table for two downtown for a date-night dinner around 8 PM."
 
Early testing showed nearly half of all restaurant orders placed through the tool were from a place the customer had never ordered from before; carts assembled through the chatbot were more than **35% higher in value** than a standard grocery order, with users completing them **five times faster**. The product draws on AI models built by OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google.


**DoorDash's 2026 Restaurant Industry Trends Report** (3,000+ US consumers, 500+ operators) surfaced the pace of AI-driven discovery: 
almost one-quarter (22%) of consumers have used an AI tool like ChatGPT or Google Gemini to help choose a restaurant. Restaurant listing sites like DoorDash account for more than **41% of the sources AI tools cite** when recommending restaurants, leading operators to update menu information (39%), manage reviews (34%), and improve photo quality (32%).
 
Three-quarters of consumers say they're comfortable using AI for reservations, but only **28% of operators are using AI to manage calls and customer service**. An estimated **40% of reservation calls go unanswered** industry-wide.



**Google** at I/O publicly committed to extending its **Universal Commerce Protocol** — the system that lets AI agents handle purchases inside Google's search and chat interfaces — to hotel booking, naming it the next vertical after restaurant reservations. UCP is expanding "to even more verticals, starting soon with hotel booking and local food delivery." The new **Agent Payments Protocol** will enable AI agents to complete purchases on users' behalf, starting with Gemini Spark AI. Google has confirmed named launch partners: Booking.com, Choice Hotels International, Expedia, IHG Hotels & Resorts, Marriott International, and Wyndham Hotels & Resorts.
 
Agentic restaurant reservations are already live in the US without a Labs opt-in. For hotels, Google VP Julie Farago told PhocusWire: "We're not going to rush this out the door because we want to make sure that it's a seamless experience." Google is in talks with additional companies.


**Markus Busch (hospitality.today)** published the sharpest structural analysis of this cycle. 
On April 28, Booking Holdings CEO Glenn Fogel told analysts the company was working closely with OpenAI and Google — named explicitly — to ensure Booking's supply is discoverable wherever travelers begin their journey. The framing was precise: not reacting to AI discovery, but engineering a position inside it.
 
Busch's thesis: AI-referred travelers who arrived with high intent and converted 28% less than other visitors were not failing to book — they were completing their journey through a conversion layer that routes to whoever holds the strongest position in it. "A traveler who finds an independent property through ChatGPT, verifies through Google, lands on an OTA listing, and books there — the hotel paid for the top of the funnel. The OTA collected at the bottom. Same as before. Different pipe."



**Booking Holdings** reported Q1 2026 net income of **$1.1 billion**, a 225% increase year-over-year, on gross bookings of **$53.8 billion** (up 15% YoY). Fogel framed AI as the primary commercial moat: the company has partnered with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Amazon to keep travelers inside Booking's ecosystem. Agoda saw a **double-digit year-over-year reduction in customer service costs per booking** driven by AI-assisted automation.


Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/11/doordashs-new-ai-chatbot-lets-you-order-with-prompts-and-photos/), [Hospitality Net / hospitality.today](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/opinion/4133170/hotels-are-building-the-ai-discovery-layer-otas-are-harvesting-it), [PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/news/online/booking-holdings-earnings-q1-2026), [Skift](https://skift.com/2026/05/19/google-names-hotels-as-next-vertical-for-agentic-shopping/)

**So what:** For restaurant operators, managing your DoorDash listing quality is now a primary AI SEO signal, not just a delivery channel. For hotel operators, the Google hotel booking launch will create a first-mover advantage for the six named partners — independent properties should assess whether their PMS can expose live rate and availability data to Google's Agent Payments Protocol before the launch, not after.

## Vendor Moves

Two platforms made their most substantive product releases in years this cycle.


**Toast** launched **Toast Drive-Thru**, an enterprise-grade platform that replaces fragmented drive-thru systems with a unified stack across trusted hardware, POS-native software, AI voice ordering integrations, and end-to-end support. Toast Drive-Thru includes AI voice ordering integrations with partners like Incept AI, with additional voice AI partners to follow in 2026.
 
Toast also launched **Toast IQ Grow**, a marketing-focused AI tool introduced as part of its Spring 2026 release of more than 20 product updates. Toast now positions IQ as an AI assistant that helps operators ask questions, surface insights, and take actions based on their own sales, labor, menu, guest, and operational data — underscoring a broader industry shift from dashboards that display data to systems that can help interpret and act on it.



Toast also announced **Toast Lab**, a hands-on collaboration where Toast will co-fund and co-develop a new Boston-area restaurant with a selected operator to pressure-test its full product suite. The winning operator receives strategic capital, early access to new Toast products, direct mentorship from Toast's executive team, and real-time support. "The best restaurant technology gets built when operators and product teams are working side-by-side," said CMO Kelly Esten.
 Applications closed July 14.

**SevenRooms** launched **Channel Connect** (from RTN news ticker, June 2026), framed as bringing hotel-style distribution management to restaurants — allowing operators to manage reservation availability across multiple consumer channels from a single system. This is the first named product release under DoorDash ownership.

**Taco Bell** (from RTN ticker, July 2026) is expanding **drive-thru voice AI to nearly 900 US restaurants** — the largest announced QSR voice AI rollout on record, validating the technology at national chain scale after McDonald's earlier, troubled trial.

Sources: [Restaurant Technology News](https://restauranttechnologynews.com/2026/06/toast-advances-restaurant-operations-with-connected-pos-ai-and-guest-engagement-technology/), [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260414359751/en/Toast-Launches-Unified-Drive-Thru-Solution-to-Modernize-Enterprise-Quick-Service-Restaurant-Operations), [Restaurant Business Online](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/technology/toast-wants-help-open-restaurant-boston), [Restaurant Technology News](https://restauranttechnologynews.com/2026/07/taco-bell-expands-drive-thru-voice-ai-to-nearly-900-restaurants-across-the-u-s-as-automation-gains-momentum/)

**So what:** Toast is executing a full-stack land-grab — drive-thru for QSR enterprise, IQ Grow for marketing automation, Toast Lab for product development credibility. For competing vendors in the restaurant tech space, the window to operate adjacent to Toast without competing against it is narrowing. Taco Bell's voice AI scale validates the technology for any QSR brand still in pilot.

## Operational AI

Back-of-house AI is moving from pilot to attributable outcome across both sectors.


**BCG's** February 2026 analysis documented that 37% of travelers already use AI large language models embedded in online travel sites to plan and book trips, establishing AI as a material inbound channel, not a future one.
 On the cost side, BCG surfaced specific operational wins: 
Ritz-Carlton San Francisco's AI room-cleaning synchronization cut prep time by 20%; IHG deployed predictive housekeeping models; Four Seasons Peninsula Papagayo cut food waste 50% within eight months using Winnow's camera-and-scale AI waste-tracking system. In economy and midscale properties, agentic AI can remove substantial friction through automated biometric check-in, robot-delivered amenities, and multilingual AI concierges.



**American Express** launched its **Dining Companion**, which uses Gen AI to help Card Members discover places to dine via Resy inventory. AmEx's published roadmap is explicit: "Today, it simplifies discovery. Tomorrow, it will go further enabling agentic booking of reservations on a Card Member's behalf." These innovations are incubated through Amex Digital Labs, with the goal of delivering "agentic commerce" experiences at scale.
 AmEx's separately published ACE Developer Kit enables third-party AI agents — Claude, ChatGPT — to complete payments with tokenization, passkey authentication, and spend controls, with Resy named as one early use case.

Sources: [BCG](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/ai-first-hotels-leaner-faster-smarter), [AmEx Newsroom](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/newsroom/articles/innovation/what-sets-american-express-apart-in-the-age-of-agentic-commerce.html)

**So what:** The Winnow food-waste case (50% reduction in 8 months, full attribution to a specific property) is the strongest operational AI case study of the cycle. Any hospitality operator targeting sustainability or F&B cost reduction should evaluate AI waste tracking before any guest-facing AI spend. AmEx's ACE Developer Kit is the infrastructure layer that will power agentic dining reservations — vendors building booking flows should assess compatibility now.

## People & Skills

The restaurant labour crisis is generating a dedicated AI hiring and retention category.


**MAJC**, launched in Miami in February, combines AI-powered job-matching, training, and community tools into a single workforce engine for hospitality professionals. The platform debuted with a culinary council led by James Beard Award winner Andrew Zimmern and Darden CPO Sarah King. The numbers behind the crisis: the average annual restaurant turnover rate topped **75%**, with QSR regularly exceeding **130%**. The NRA's 2026 State of the Industry report found **80% of restaurants were short at least one position in 2025**, with the average operator carrying five open roles (up from 3.8).


The most validated AI hiring case study: 
multilingual AI-powered hiring tools reduced **Chipotle's** application-to-start date from **12 days to 4**, nearly doubled applicant flow, and pushed application completion rates from roughly **50% to nearly 85%**.



A TD Bank survey of restaurant franchise leaders found that labor efficiency, training, and scheduling were the top areas where AI could be of use (40%), with customer data analysis and trend predictions (34%) and customer experience/personalization (28%) not far behind.


Sources: [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/restaurants-cannot-find-workers-ai-says-it-can/), [Nation's Restaurant News](https://www.nrn.com/restaurant-labor/labor-shortages-dominate-restaurant-concerns-for-2026-but-ai-could-provide-relief-survey-says)

**So what:** Chipotle's AI hiring numbers (12-to-4-day cycle, 85% completion rate) are signal, not noise — specific, attributed, and institutionally verified. For multi-unit restaurant operators, AI hiring platforms have faster and more measurable ROI than most guest-facing AI investments. MAJC's integrated workforce approach (matching + training + community) is worth monitoring as a platform investment candidate.
