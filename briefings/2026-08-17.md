## AI Visibility & Discovery

The long-promised agentic booking layer became a live product for both restaurants and hotels in the same 96-hour window.


On 6 August, Google announced that its "Ask Maps" feature inside Google Maps was gaining agentic capabilities, including the ability to order food, book hotels, and find event tickets. The tech giant also brought Personal Intelligence to Ask Maps, allowing the AI tool to personalize its responses by drawing on information from a user's Gmail and Google Calendar.
 
For food ordering, after selecting a restaurant, users can tap "Order online" to place an order through supported platforms such as Square, Toast, or Uber Eats — Ask Maps adds items to a cart and the user completes payment on the supported platform.



On 7 August, Google launched a limited U.S. test of agentic hotel booking within Search's AI Mode, with Booking Holdings among the first partners and Expedia, Marriott, Wyndham, and IHG also collaborating. Google's new Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) could allow bookings to be completed directly inside a chat session — Amadeus is its first B2B foundational partner for lodging, with Sabre advising on the air side.
 
Booking Holdings CEO Glenn Fogel said on an earnings call: "I think Google may have come out today, a small percentage of their traffic seeing their agentic booking … we are one of the first with them."



This traffic finding has not, at this stage, translated into a measurable shift in bookings. Booking Holdings CFO Ewout Steenbergen said in Q2 2026 that bookings coming from conversational agents, free or paid, account for less than 1% of the group's room-nights, with no significant recent change in that share.


**OpenTable, Resy, and Yelp inside ChatGPT.** 
On August 10, 2026, Yelp announced that ChatGPT users can now access Yelp Reservations and Waitlist to book a table or join a queue at thousands of restaurants across the US and Canada without leaving the chat. Resy, owned by American Express, launched Resy Reservations in ChatGPT for US restaurants the same week. OpenTable powers recommendations globally.
 The architecture inference from the explainx.ai analysis: the rendered interactive booking card matching the pattern of OpenAI's Apps SDK, which is built on the Model Context Protocol — the same underlying standard that allowed Resy to simultaneously go live in Claude (via the April AmEx/Anthropic partnership). One platform, two assistants, same week: that is what a protocol looks like from the outside.


The integration arrived 17 days after Yelp licensed its reviews, photos and business information to OpenAI. Yelp Host had already disclosed more than one million calls handled since October 2025 and average month-over-month growth of 38%. The precedent for in-chat transactions is mixed: OpenAI discontinued its Instant Checkout feature in March 2026 after Walmart's publicly disclosed data showed conversion rates for products sold inside the chatbot ran three times lower than for purchases requiring a click out to Walmart's own site. Restaurant reservations, however, involve no payment or shipping — a materially different friction profile.



An Originality.ai study published in May 2026 found only 26 public websites had implemented the Universal Commerce Protocol out of more than three million scanned.
 The infrastructure is real; adoption is embryonic.

**Booking Holdings and Google AI Overviews.** 
Booking is accelerating its AI strategy across customer service, AI discovery, and LLM visibility, while raising its transformation-program savings target to $650 million in annual run-rate savings by 2027. CEO Fogel told analysts: "No travel business has found the silver bullet yet in how to show up prominently on a consistent basis within the major LLMs."


Sources: [Skift – Google hotel booking test](https://skift.com/2026/08/07/google-confirms-hotel-agentic-booking-is-now-in-testing/) · [TechCrunch – Ask Maps agentic](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/google-maps-adds-agentic-features-including-food-ordering-and-hotel-bookings/) · [explainx.ai – ChatGPT restaurant bookings](https://explainx.ai/blog/chatgpt-restaurant-reservations-opentable-resy-yelp-august-2026) · [Skift – Booking Q2](https://skift.com/2026/08/04/booking-holdings-saw-pressure-from-googles-ai-overviews/)

**So what:** Discovery is now inseparable from transaction. A restaurant or hotel that cannot be booked from inside ChatGPT, Claude, or Google Maps is absent from the moment of decision for a growing share of diners and travellers. The practical ask for mid-market operators is narrow and urgent: ensure your reservation inventory is live and accurate on at least one platform that has an active AI-surface deal (OpenTable, Resy, Yelp Guest Manager, SevenRooms/DoorDash). A listing that can't surface a live slot won't transact.

## Vendor Moves

The biggest structural story of 2026 is the collapse of category boundaries in restaurant and hotel tech — payments, delivery, and reservations are becoming one thing.

**American Express** is assembling the largest restaurant reservation empire in history. 
American Express has proposed acquiring European restaurant reservation platform TheFork from Tripadvisor for $700 million in cash, adding more than 50,000 restaurants across 11 European countries to its dining portfolio. American Express estimates that TheFork, Resy, and Tock would together create a dining network of approximately 75,000 bookable venues.
 Simultaneously, 
American Express is combining its two existing reservation platforms into one under Resy this summer, bringing Tock's inventory of fine-dining restaurants and wineries to Resy for the first time.
 Resy CEO Pablo Rivero's stated vision: 
a powerful tech stack that links AI-powered discovery and booking, table and guestbook management, content and curation, ticketing and transactions, check-level point of sale data, and American Express membership.


**DoorDash / SevenRooms** launched reservations across Los Angeles, San Francisco and Boston, 
and via SevenRooms is also offering restaurants voice AI that can take reservations over the phone and event management capabilities including group bookings and private dining.
 
SevenRooms also unveiled Channel Connect, a desktop app that aggregates reservations from various booking channels into a single system while automatically updating a restaurant's inventory across those channels — described by CEO Joel Montaniel as "more tables for diners, one book for restaurants."
 
Channel Connect can access reservations from "major booking platforms" — meaning rivals OpenTable and Resy — using credentials and data the restaurant already controls. Restaurants own the customer data within Channel Connect.
 This is a direct response to OpenTable's April 2026 system-of-record mandate.

**Deliveroo** (owned by DoorDash) 
launched Deliveroo Reservations through its integration with SevenRooms, enabling diners to book restaurant tables directly from within the delivery application, initially rolling out across London with plans to expand across the UK.


**Toast** reported Q2 2026 results on 10 August. 
Toast's first AI-enabled workflow product — a digital marketing tool called Toast IQ Grow — is poised to become the company's fastest-growing product, with executives expecting it to generate $10 million in annual revenue since its May introduction. CEO Aman Narang said most Toast customers are unable to leverage the company's various offerings and often outsource major functions such as marketing, payroll and bookkeeping.
 
Narang added that Toast IQ Grow's early success strengthens management's confidence that AI can increase the amount customers spend on the platform over time. A Louisiana restaurant, Spirits Food & Friends, reduced its outside marketing costs by 70% while generating more than $100,000 in marketing-attributed sales in less than two months.
 
Toast added a record 9,500 new locations in the quarter, for a total of about 180,000, and announced deals with BWH Hotels (Best Western parent) and Kung Fu Tea. Annualized recurring run-rate rose 25% to $2.4 billion.


**Tabbed** announced its integration with the Toast Partner Ecosystem on 10 August. 
Tabbed's AI can read each live check and surface smart suggestions to the server right on the Toast POS system: a wine that pairs with the table's entrées, the right moment for a second round, a dessert worth staying for. A single restaurant using Tabbed cut payment-related service time by more than nine minutes per table, turning tables faster, with average tips up 24%.


Sources: [NRN – SevenRooms Channel Connect](https://www.nrn.com/restaurant-technology/sevenrooms-launches-a-reservations-aggregator-for-restaurants) · [Restaurant Dive – Toast AI](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/toast-aims-to-drive-ai-into-dining/827503/) · [Mozrest – Platform expansion analysis](https://mozrest.com/en-gb/restaurant-tech-insights/restaurant-technology-platforms/) · [NRN – TheFork / Resy+Tock](https://www.nrn.com/restaurant-technology/tech-tracker-a-burst-of-m-a-to-start-the-summer)

**So what:** The reservation platform is no longer a booking tool — it is a data infrastructure that controls guest identity across delivery, dine-in, and AI discovery. Picking a reservation partner now means picking an ecosystem: DoorDash's delivery reach vs. AmEx's affluent cardholder base vs. Booking Holdings' OTA machinery. SevenRooms' Channel Connect is the most structurally interesting move — free, open-license aggregation is a direct attack on OpenTable's gatekeeper position.

## Funding & M&A


Between April 2025 and March 2026, startups across the hospitality technology sector pulled in more than $1 billion in funding across 40 companies, with property management systems and AI-led platforms claiming the biggest slice. PMS companies led with a combined $408.1 million raised across seven players, while AI-driven guest experience platforms brought in $152.6 million.



The best-funded PMS businesses are using their position to build broader platforms through product development and acquisition rather than relying on third-party integrations. Mews' acquisitions of Flexkeeping and DataChat in late 2025 are one example. AI-led guest experience platforms — Duve, Chatlyn, Conduit, and Canary Technologies — raised a combined $152.6 million, targeting the need to deliver fast, personalised service with leaner teams.



Jessica Gillingham, CEO of Abode Worldwide, said: "The real story is where the money is going. Investors are concentrating capital in the platforms that hospitality businesses increasingly depend on every day, especially PMS and AI-led systems that unify operations, improve automation and strengthen the data layer underneath the business. Unified systems generate more data, better automation and higher switching costs. This is a compounding dynamic that is proving more appealing to investors."


Sources: [Hotel Dive – $1B funding report](https://www.hoteldive.com/news/hospitality-tech-attracts-1b-in-funding-pms-ai-leaders/817439/) · [WiT – Abode Worldwide analysis](https://www.webintravel.com/hospitality-tech-hit-a-billion-dollar-milestone-heres-where-the-smart-money-is-going/)

**So what:** The PE-backed SaaS thesis is clear: own the data layer, expand laterally through acquisition, create switching costs before the agentic booking wave fully arrives. For mid-market operators evaluating PMS replacements, the question to add to the RFP is "what data does this system make readable to external AI agents — and on whose terms?"

## Governance & Security

HITEC 2026 in San Antonio marked the moment hotel AI governance shifted from background discussion to front-of-stage urgency. 
As hotels begin deploying dozens of AI-powered workflows across revenue management, marketing, reservations, maintenance, finance and guest communications, the challenge is taking stock and reining in all these agents. The most important emerging software category from HITEC 2026 is what Adam Mogelonsky called the Agent Management Platform (AMP) — a governance layer that controls permissions, monitors performance, manages security and tracks costs. "The winners will not necessarily be the organizations deploying the most agents. They will be the ones that know exactly which agents are operating, what they are allowed to do, how much they cost and how effectively they contribute to business performance."



Token costs are about to become a very real part of the tech stack opex budget. As AI adoption accelerates, hotels will discover that thousands of automated decisions, guest interactions, forecasts, reports and content generation tasks create a new operating expense category.


The regulatory stack is tightening simultaneously. 
The EU AI Act Article 50 transparency requirements for chatbots apply from 2 August 2026. GDPR governs the processing of personal data in AI interactions for EU residents.
 
On June 2, 2026, the White House issued an Executive Order titled "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security," directing federal bodies to prioritize AI cyber defense and creating an AI cybersecurity clearinghouse.
 Terence Ronson's analysis for Hospitality Net argues the implications cascade: 
the Order extends AI-enabled cybersecurity services to operators of critical infrastructure, and over time hospitality environments that support large-scale economic activity may find themselves drawn into broader resilience and cybersecurity expectations — through regulation, insurance, or brand-standard pressure.



Hotel operators are now being pushed toward more structured AI governance frameworks. Under GDPR compliance requirements, guest data must be processed lawfully and transparently; regulators are paying closer attention to how that data is later used in AI models, particularly when shared with third-party technology providers. Automated systems can analyse past stays, browsing behaviour, and spending patterns in ways that constitute "automated decision-making," which in some cases requires explicit user safeguards.


Sources: [HospitalityNet – HITEC 2026 governance](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/opinion/4133373/agentic-governance-is-the-big-trend-from-hitec-2026) · [HospitalityNet – White House EO for hotels](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/opinion/4132771/when-national-ai-security-becomes-a-hospitality-imperative)

**So what:** The practical first step for any operator is an AI exposure audit: list every system that uses AI, what guest data it touches, what actions it can take autonomously. Do this before the insurer asks for it in the next policy cycle. The AMP category is early-stage — no dominant vendor — but knowing the concept now means you can evaluate vendors entering this space rather than discovering you need one during an incident.

## Data & Integration

The commission question in agentic booking remains entirely unsettled. 
In October, Booking and Expedia launched apps inside ChatGPT reaching its 800-million-plus user base, while Google struck a payments deal with PayPal. The clearest map of where this leaves hotels reduces agentic booking to three competing models: the familiar OTA wrapped in a chat interface (same commission, new storefront); the MCP aggregator that introduces a new toll that didn't exist before; and a hotel-owned MCP server that talks directly to the assistant with no intermediary.



While the commission model stays unsettled, monetization is quietly migrating elsewhere: advertising inside the chat itself. ChatGPT's ads, launched in February 2026, already have Expedia buying and Marriott piloting. Tellingly, neither Google nor OpenAI wants to be the merchant of record on the transaction — both would rather the partner handle payment and keep its usual economics, while the platforms position themselves where the actual fight is: control of the conversation with the traveler.


The hotel data layer problem is deeper than distribution. 
Every major hotel chain has now committed publicly to an agentic AI roadmap — Marriott's $1B+ agentic mesh, Hilton's AI Planner, Hyatt and Accor inside ChatGPT, IHG's AI-enhanced CRM. All of them are bottlenecked at the same layer: the property management system was built when a guest was a stay at a property, not a relationship across the chain.


Sources: [HospitalityNet – Commission question](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/opinion/4133767/the-agentic-booking-question-no-one-has-answered-who-gets-the-commission) · [hospitality-on.com – Google hotel booking test](https://hospitality-on.com/en/distribution/google-launches-ai-agent-hotel-booking-test-united-states)

**So what:** Before signing any MCP integration, operators should know which of the three commission models they are entering. The only wrong answer, as one analyst put it, is not knowing which one you've made.

## Operational AI

**Toast** is the clearest restaurant-sector signal: 
Narang said Toast will deploy "agentic products" atop its software for demand forecasting, labor schedules, income tax help, food costs and availability — "an incredibly exciting future, especially in an industry known for slim margins and long hours."
 
The company also launched Toast Drive-Thru in 2026 as a unified solution for enterprise quick-service operations, bringing together hardware, POS-native software, AI voice ordering integrations and services.


Industry-wide, 
twenty-six percent of restaurant operators say they are using AI-related tools at their restaurants, according to the National Restaurant Association's State of the Restaurant Industry 2026 report.
 
Eight in ten restaurant executives say their investments in AI technologies will increase in the next fiscal year. Identifying the right use cases and managing risks are top challenges, alongside a lack of technical talent, compliance concerns, and a lack of governance.


The most grounded practitioner case study in this cycle: 
Casper Hospitality put Voice AI on the phones and in one month saw: 3,800+ calls handled, 850+ covers created, $28K booked revenue reported in the 2026 Trends Report.


Sources: [Restaurant Dive – Toast](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/toast-aims-to-drive-ai-into-dining/827503/) · [SevenRooms – Voice AI case study](https://sevenrooms.com/blog/restaurant-AI/)

**So what:** Voice AI for reservations and phone ordering has cleared the proof-of-concept bar; it now has outcome data at scale. For independent operators, this is the lowest-friction AI entry point: no POS replacement required, immediate revenue capture from missed calls.
